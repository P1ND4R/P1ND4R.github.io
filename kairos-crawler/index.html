KAIROS-CI crawler

If you found this page in your server logs, a request came from the KAIROS-CI crawler. This page explains what it does and how to stop it.

What it is

KAIROS-CI is an early-stage competitive intelligence project for small local businesses in the United States. It reads public marketing pages of local service businesses (for example, which services, offers or financing options a practice advertises) and summarizes changes over time.

How to recognize it

Requests use this User-Agent:

KAIROS-CI/<version> (+https://p1nd4r.github.io/kairos-crawler/)

How it behaves

  • It follows robots.txt. If your robots.txt cannot be read (for example, due to an error or a block), it does not fetch your site at all in that run.
  • It honours Crawl-delay, waits at least 10 seconds between requests to the same site, and fetches only a handful of pages per site per run.
  • It fetches only public HTML pages. It never submits forms, logs in, books appointments or uses cookies.
  • It does not try to get around bot protection. A block or a rate limit is respected and recorded as a miss.
  • It does not collect patient, customer or other personal data.

How to block it

Add this to your robots.txt:

User-agent: KAIROS-CI
Disallow: /

The crawler will stop fetching your site from its next run.

Contact

To ask a question, report a problem or request that your site is excluded, open an issue here: github.com/P1ND4R/P1ND4R.github.io/issues. Exclusion requests are honoured without question.
